# Works pages: the basis

Every section of `/works/[slug]` is grounded in two sources. Nothing is placed or written without one of them.

1. **The AE boards**: `2026_Port_Static/MainTitle.aep`, folder `NewComps` (the latest board set), read 2026-11-11
   through AfterEffectsMCP (layer sources and text layers). Rendered stills: `MainTitle.aep_AME/`.
2. **The Behance case study**: behance.net/gallery/256515845 (module order and captions).

Source footage lives in `2026_Port_Static/All_Assets`, `GIF_cutter/` (the loops cut for the boards) and the
project folders the comps link to (`D:/Works/HerondLabs/...`, `D:/Works/Showcase/ZCungViet/...`).

## Board grammar (what the web pages replay)

| Board | What it does | Web |
|---|---|---|
| Info (`04`, `07 Info 2`, `10 Info`) | Hairline rule, then the role as a **sans lead** (Product Sans 128/2800), the intro body under it, a collage of the work beside it. The project's chapters are set as a **serif list** (Lastik 160/2800) with staggered indents, on a fountain column (`04`) or a band (`10`, ink ramp). | `editorial-text`: lead + body + chapter list. The chapter list is the page's table of contents. |
| Content (`10.2 Orb1`, `Rewards`, `Rewards 2`, `UIFilmBreakdown`) | **No headline.** The media is the composition (a medal ring around the orb, a hand holding the orb, in-app screens floating on a blob). Body copy is small sans (36/2800) set in an empty corner of paper, sometimes split into two blocks at opposite corners. Step labels are small sans under each clip. | Section modules: the copy is a corner note, the chapter is a small running label, never a big title. |
| Intro (`02`) | A huge defocused display word behind a fountain card of sans body; a serif "Contents" over a hairline and a centred serif list. | Big/small type contrast only in this form: one display word or name, defocused, under crisp sans. Never a paragraph in the serif. |
| End (`05`, `08`, `11`) | Overprint stack of the name, sans lead, body columns. | `zine-outro`. The name is never covered by media or copy. |

Rules that follow:
- Display serif is for **names and chapter words**, never for running sentences.
- Sans lead is for **the role line** only.
- Body copy is small sans, in a paper field, beside or under the work.
- Big type is never overprinted by media or copy (user, 2026-11-11).
